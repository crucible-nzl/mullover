# Appropriate keywords · classified from the 2026-08-16 Keyword Planner export

Source: `Keyword Stats 2026-08-16 at 10_11_55.csv` (NZ geo, NZD bids, Aug 2025
to Jul 2026 averages). 2,321 keywords, 549 with reported volume. Volumes are
Planner buckets (~50 / ~500 / ~5,000 / ~50,000), so treat them as tiers, not
counts. Bid ranges are NZD top-of-page estimates.

Classification rule: a keyword earns a place only if (a) the searcher is
weighing a decision we genuinely serve, (b) the click won't be outbid by an
industry with a thousand times our budget, and (c) it crosses none of our
policy lines (no clinical, no financial advice, no crisis interception).

★ = switch on first · the launch set.

  1.32-16.21). Real fit, pharma-funded auction. Only after the drinking
  group proves the copy pattern.
